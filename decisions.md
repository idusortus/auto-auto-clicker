## 2026-09-26 — Review finding: derived gear stats are a cache, recompute on load
**Context:** The drops-primary reversal changed the gear stat formula (power law → exponential) while `SaveGame.version` stayed 1. `GearInstance.dps`/`clickDamage` are a derived cache persisted inside `GameState`, and `loadGame` (`state.ts`) clones the blob without recomputing them. A version-1 save written by the previous build therefore loads with stale cached stats until the next equip/upgrade (probe: an itemLevel-30 weapon loads `dps=84` vs the new formula's `2751`). The web host reads the cache directly through `getEffectiveStats`, so the stale value drives combat and the HUD.
**Choice:** Treat persisted derived stats as a cache, not truth: `loadGame` normalizes (recomputes) `dps`/`clickDamage` from `computeGearStats` for the equipped item and every bag item, resolving each `definitionId` through `gearDefinitionFor` (falling back to `WEAPON_DEFINITION`, matching `actions.ts`). `CURRENT_SAVE_VERSION` stays 1 — it tracks the save *schema*, so a balance/formula change alone is not a schema bump; the persisted shape is unchanged and only the derived cache values are normalized on load.
**Trade-offs:** Recomputation adds a small per-load cost and means a save is no longer a byte-exact round-trip of the writer's derived fields (the schema shape is unchanged; only cache values are normalized).
**Status:** IMPLEMENTED — `normalizeGearInstance` in `state.ts`; covered by a `save.test.ts` case that loads a deliberately-wrong `dps` in both the equipped slot and the bag.
**Revisit:** If a future balance change alters item-level/upgrade semantics again, confirm load-time recomputation keeps old saves coherent rather than silently reinterpreting them.

## 2026-09-26 — Economy reversal: drops are the primary power lever, gold demoted
**Context:** The Phase 3b "deterministic power core" made gear drops power-neutral after the guaranteed starter weapon so every sweep seed produced identical timings. The user reversed that decision directly: gear DROPS must be the primary driver of power growth, with gold-funded upgrades demoted to a minor smoothing lever. The old design could not satisfy it structurally — gear stat was a power law in item level (`dpsFactor * itemLevel^levelExponent`) while enemy HP is exponential (`hpGrowth^stage`), and drops trailed the stage by `DROP_LEVEL_OFFSET = 6` at `dropChance = 0.08`, so a polynomial lever could never drive an exponential curve.
**Choice:** Gear stats are now EXPONENTIAL in item level (`floor(factor * gearGrowth^(itemLevel - 1))`, same for click damage) with `gearGrowth = 1.283`; `GearDefinition.levelExponent` is replaced by `gearGrowth` (content, not save state). `dropChance` is 0.95 and `DROP_LEVEL_OFFSET` is 0, so a killed stage reliably yields a weapon whose item level tracks that stage (`itemLevel = max(1, stage)`) — a modest integer in lockstep with the stage. Gold is a minor lever: `upgradeStatMultiplier = 1.05`, `upgradeCostGrowth = 6`, flat gold (`goldGrowth = 1.0`), so only ≈0.7 upgrade levels are affordable per equip, and equipping a new drop resets `upgradeLevel` to 0 — cheap relative to the ≈28% item-level jump. `hpGrowth` is 1.42 so player power (≈1.28×/stage) falls behind enemy HP (1.42×/stage) at the designed ratio; the stage-30 boss is the soft check and the stage-50 boss the hard wall. The sim now carries a power-attribution ledger: the equipped stat's log decomposes exactly into per-equip `Δ(itemLevel − 1)·ln(gearGrowth)` plus per-upgrade `+ln(upgradeStatMultiplier)`, computed only from exported engine values (`BALANCE`). The reset loss from each equip is charged to gold (`goldNet = goldGross − resetLoss`), never subtracted from drops, so gold's net contribution is ≈0 and drops carry ≈100% of net log-power growth. A drops-primary gate — drops must exceed 50% of positive *net* log-power growth — is a hard per-seed assertion alongside the unchanged ±20% pacing windows, plus a drop-stream sanity guard (equips must keep pace with stages cleared).
**Trade-offs:** Drop RNG now influences pacing, so the 5-seed sweep is a real robustness probe instead of a determinism guard; `dropChance` 0.95 keeps the spread tight (soft 6.39–6.52 min, hard 50.78–51.38 min, drops ≈100% net / 87.6% gross of log growth; `goldGross = resetLoss = 1.659`, so `goldNet = 0`), but a dry spell can still shift a crossing — at `dropChance` 0.8 the observed soft range blows out to 2.33–8.75 min. Determinism and seed-robustness are the explicit price of a drops-primary economy. `goldGrowth = 1.0` makes late-game gold flat and, with steep costs, gold is a small lever (≈0% of NET log growth — the upgrade power it buys is destroyed by the resets; 12.4% gross; the free `wait` grant ≈4.5%). The `/web` smoke test's "keep upgrading until DPS rises" assertion encoded the old economy and was repointed at equipping a higher-level drop; `/web/src` is unchanged and still holds no balance numbers.
**Revisit:** If drop-RNG pacing variance becomes unacceptable, raise `dropChance` toward 1.0 (or add a per-stage pity counter) — never widen the ±20% tolerance and never re-neuter drops. If gold should be a larger lever again, that is a new design decision, not a tuning tweak.

## 2026-09-25 — Orchestrator: accept determinism-by-construction for the pacing proof
**Context:** Phase 3's sim passed on the canonical seed, but 4/5 sweep seeds missed the ±20% window by 30–75%. The pacing was a knife-edge crossing of two near-equal exponentials (player power ≈1.384×/stage vs enemy HP 1.5×/stage), decided by drop RNG, and the curve was degenerate (soft check fired at stage 70 by 6.6 min with ~1e18 DPS). The brief forbids loosening the target and requires stopping/reporting if the target is only reachable with an insane economy — so the fragile canonical pass was escalated to the user instead of shipped.
**Choice:** The user chose a full economy redesign over accepting a canonical-only proof. Power is now driven by gold-funded upgrades, gear drops are power-neutral after a guaranteed starter weapon, free-path (`wait`/`watchAd`) grants are bounded to a fixed number of upgrade levels, and the walls come from designed boss-HP gates (soft at the stage-30 boss, hard at stage 60). The 5-seed sweep was tightened from informational warnings to a HARD all-seed assertion, and `BOSS_TIMER_MS` / the ±20% tolerance were left untouched.
**Trade-offs:** All seeds now produce identical timings, so the sweep is a determinism/regression guard rather than a robustness probe, and gear drops no longer influence pacing at all — the "gear drops → upgrade gear" step is real but low-impact. In exchange the proof is deterministic, auditable, and still sensitive to every economy lever that matters.
**Revisit:** If drops must be a meaningful pacing lever, co-design `balance.ts` + `loot.ts` (a bounded DPS delta per drop) and add a loot-sensitivity assertion — never widen the ±20% tolerance.
**Superseded by:** "Economy reversal: drops are the primary power lever, gold demoted" (2026-09-26) — the user reversed the determinism-first design; drops are the power lever again.

## 2026-09-25 — Boundary hardening from the design review (targeted fixes)
**Context:** An independent review passed the build with notes and found four real gaps: engine-core's tsconfig default-injected the DOM lib, the fs boundary test did not scan for DOM/RN globals, `advance` let non-finite `deltaMs` poison state, and `loadGame` threw a raw `TypeError` for a version-1 blob with a missing `state`. `sim/` was also outside every typecheck, and a few doc claims overstated the seed sweep and gear-drop impact.
**Choice:** (1) `engine-core/tsconfig.json` sets `"lib": ["ES2022"]`; a probe confirmed `document` no longer typechecks. (2) `boundary.test.ts` blanks comments (and string/template literals for identifier checks) then rejects `document`/`window`/`react`/`react-native`/`jsdom` as identifiers, plus anchored `from`/`import`/`require` patterns for the module names. (3) `advance` guards `!Number.isFinite(deltaMs) || deltaMs <= 0`. (4) `loadGame` validates that `state` is a non-null, non-array object for a supported version and throws a descriptive error. (5) `sim/tsconfig.json` (`extends` the base, `include: ["src"]`, `types: ["node"]`) plus a `typecheck` script, and root `typecheck` now runs all three workspaces. (6) `web/src/renderer.ts` `hpPercent` returns 0 for non-finite HP. README/decisions wording now calls the seed sweep a determinism/regression guard (not a loot-robustness probe) and states that drops are power-neutral after the guaranteed starter weapon; the carry range is `[0, 1)`.
**Trade-offs:** The boundary scanner strips template-literal interpolation along with string text, so a DOM identifier hidden inside `${...}` would not be caught — acceptable for the prototype; ordinary statement/property usage is covered and was probe-verified.
**Revisit:** If a future adapter genuinely needs a DOM type, declare a local structural interface rather than re-adding the DOM lib.

## 2026-09-25 — engine-core DOM-lib guard is documented but not implemented (review finding)
**Context:** README ("engine-core purity contract") and the "Async SaveRepository + structural localStorage adapter" entry below both claim `engine-core/tsconfig` has no DOM lib. A review probe shows otherwise: a file referencing `document`/`window` typechecks under `engine-core/tsconfig.json` because `lib` is unset and TypeScript default-injects `DOM` for a given target. The fs-based boundary test only bans `Math.random`, `Date.now`, `performance.now`, `setTimeout`, `fetch`, and `web` imports — not `document`, `window`, or `react`/`react-native`.
**Choice:** Add `"lib": ["ES2022"]` to `engine-core/tsconfig.json` and extend `engine-core/tests/boundary.test.ts` to reject `document`, `window`, and React/React-Native imports. Verified all engine sources compile with the DOM lib removed.
**Trade-offs:** None material; `save/localStorage.ts` already reads storage structurally through `globalThis`, so it needs no DOM types.
**Status:** RESOLVED — see the "Boundary hardening from the design review" entry above.
**Revisit:** If a future adapter genuinely needs a DOM type, declare a local structural interface instead of re-adding the DOM lib.

## 2026-09-25 — Playwright + Chromium for the /web mobile smoke test (Phase 5)
**Context:** `/web` needed a real-browser verification of the mobile experience (tap-to-damage, earn→upgrade, 44px touch targets, clean console). engine-core's vitest suite and the headless sim cover rules and pacing, but neither exercises DOM events, touch emulation, viewport layout, or the Vite dev-server boot path.
**Choice:** Add `@playwright/test` (devDependency of `web`) as the only tool that provides real mobile-viewport + touch emulation. `web/playwright.config.ts` starts the real Vite dev server (`npm run dev -- --port 5173 --strictPort`, `reuseExistingServer: !CI`) and drives Chromium at 390×844 / `isMobile` / `hasTouch` / DPR 3. `web/tests/smoke.spec.ts` clears `localStorage` per test (the app autosaves), then asserts: core UI visible, tap strictly reduces enemy HP, earning gold enables the upgrade which increments the counter and spends gold (and eventually raises the DPS readout), enemy/upgrade controls are ≥44px in both dimensions, `viewportSize()` is 390×844, and no console/page errors fire. Root `npm run smoke` maps to the web workspace and propagates the exit code.
**Trade-offs:** Playwright is a heavyweight dev-only dependency and adds ~3s to CI; the Chromium build was already cached at `~/.cache/ms-playwright` (v1243) so no download was needed here, but a fresh machine will download one. The suite runs a single worker against the shared dev server for deterministic output, so it is not parallelised.
**Revisit:** If a faster gate is needed, run Chromium headless-shell only; if the app moves behind Supabase auth, add a storage-state fixture instead of clearing localStorage.

## 2026-09-25 — Visual system for /web: ember-on-blue-black instrument HUD (Phase 4b)
**Context:** Phase 4 shipped a correct but plain renderer. Phase 4b is CSS-only polish under hard constraints: no frameworks, no dependencies, no network requests (system fonts only), no DOM/class/testid changes, and a game-HUD feel rather than a dashboard.
**Choice:** One restrained dark theme: blue-black housing (`#08090c` page, `#171b24` panels) with a single ember accent (`#ff9d3c`) and blood red (`#ff4d5e`) reserved exclusively for enemy HP. Two system-only type roles: a system sans for labels/prose/buttons and a system monospace for *every numeral* (HUD readouts, HP, costs, gear stats) — the instrument-readout language of a numbers game. The HUD is one bordered "instrument strip" with hairline dividers rather than three separate cards, so it does not read as a SaaS card kit. The arena (enemy) is the single bold element: scanline + vignette background, quarter-tick segmented HP meter that animates depletion, and a radial hit-spark on `:active`. Boss state is derived purely from the renderer's existing `hidden` toggle on the badge via `.enemy:has(.enemy__badge:not([hidden]))`; the wall-choice overlay becomes a bottom sheet via `.overlay:has(.overlay__note)` for thumb reach. Primary buttons are dark ink on amber (not white on accent), 56px min-height; disabled states use a dashed border + recessive ink so they read unambiguously disabled. `prefers-reduced-motion` swaps transform press feedback for brightness, and `prefers-contrast: more` raises line/text contrast.
**Trade-offs:** Structural state selectors (`:has()`) depend on the skeleton's fixed shape; if the renderer later changes which element carries the badge/note hook, boss and sheet styling degrade silently (gracefully, to the neutral default). HUD values are clamped (`clamp(17px, 5.4vw, 21px)`) and wrap at ~9 digits, accepting a rare two-line readout late-game over unreadable truncation.
**Revisit:** If Stage/boss semantics change or the renderer grows a distinct boss container, replace the `:has()` derivations with an explicit class rendered by `renderer.ts`.

## 2026-09-25 — Deterministic power core: bounded RNG, bounded free grants, DPS-based swap (Phase 3b)
**Context:** Phase 3's pacing was a knife-edge crossing of two near-equal exponentials (player ≈1.384×/stage vs enemy HP 1.5×/stage), amplified by drop RNG: the canonical seed passed but 4/5 sweep seeds missed by 30–75%, the soft check fired at stage 70 after 6.6 min, and DPS reached ~1e18. The acceptance criteria now require ALL sweep seeds inside ±20% with the soft check on a boss at stage ≤ 40 and hard wall ≤ 90.
**Choice:** Four structural changes: (1) player power is dominated by gold-funded upgrades — `upgradeStatMultiplier` 1.3, `levelExponent` 1.1, `hpGrowth` 1.45, `goldGrowth` 1.35 give player power ≈1.28–1.30×/stage vs enemy HP 1.45×/stage, a growing fall-behind rather than a near-tie; (2) gear drops do not contribute player power after the guaranteed starter weapon — `dropChance` 0.08 and item level `max(1, stage - DROP_LEVEL_OFFSET)` with `DROP_LEVEL_OFFSET = 6` keep a drop bounded at ≤~3× base DPS instead of stage-proportional, so an informed player never equips a later drop over the upgraded starter; the first weapon is guaranteed (`ownsWeapon` check in `rollGearDrop`) so an uninformed player cannot be unarmed forever; (3) `resolveChoice('wait'|'watchAd')` grants `choiceGoldGrant(currentUpgradeLevel, levels)` — exactly `WAIT_UPGRADE_GRANT_LEVELS = 2` / `WATCH_AD_UPGRADE_GRANT_LEVELS = 4` levels of `upgradeCost`, bounded and stage-independent (`watchAd` strictly > `wait` strictly > nothing); (4) the sim equips a bag weapon only when it strictly raises total active DPS (`autoDps + ACTIVE_CLICKS_PER_SECOND * clickDamage`), never on `itemLevel`, so a level-0 drop cannot reset the upgrade curve. `bossHpMultiplier` 2.25 makes the stage-30 boss the designed soft gate and the stage-60 boss the hard gate. All engine-core tests that pinned live balance values were refactored to synthesize state (explicit `baseAutoDps`/`clickDamage`/`enemyHp`) or assert through `BALANCE`/formula helpers.
**Trade-offs:** `bossHpMultiplier` is a *smaller* absolute number than before, but it still needs to be a real odd-multiple gate: raising it further (≥3) makes the stage-10/20 bosses fire the soft check too early (verified: M=3 → soft 2.5 min at stage 20) and makes free-lunch accumulation dominate, so 2.25 is near the top of the feasible range. Late-game DPS at the hard wall is still ~3e7, large but finite and ordered. RNG no longer affects pacing at all, so the 5 sweep seeds are identical by construction — this is the intended outcome (deterministic power core) and removes the predictive power of the "sweep" as a robustness probe against balance drift.
**Revisit:** If boss-HP gates need to be much stronger, redesign the HP curve (e.g. an explicit boss-only tier) rather than raising `bossHpMultiplier`, since the early free stage run sets the stage-10 boss budget.
**Superseded by:** "Economy reversal: drops are the primary power lever, gold demoted" (2026-09-26) — gear drops contribute power again and are now the primary lever; the constants changed.

## 2026-09-25 — Web host owns the clock, loop, and offline replay; renderer is a pure state projection
**Context:** Phase 4 wires `/web` to the pure engine. The engine refuses clocks, DOM, and persistence timing, so the host must supply them without leaking rules or balance numbers back across the boundary.
**Choice:** `web/src/main.ts` owns `Date.now()`, `requestAnimationFrame` with an accumulator, fixed 100 ms `advance()` steps, a 10-step catch-up clamp, 5 s autosave plus `visibilitychange`/`pagehide` flushes, and offline replay. Offline time is replayed through the SAME `advance()` in bounded 1000 ms steps capped at 8 h (auto-DPS only, stops at a pending choice) rather than granting a special reward. `web/src/renderer.ts` is a stateless projection of `GameState` (plus a small allocation-diff cache keyed by gear/bag signatures); it reads only engine-core state or exported getters and forwards input through handlers. `web/src/storage.ts` is the only module that touches the `SaveRepository` and the save clock. `watchAd`/`iap` render as disabled placeholders so the free `wait` path is always available.
**Trade-offs:** Backgrounded-tab time beyond the 10-step clamp is dropped (not replayed) until the next boot, which under-counts idle progress versus a strictly wall-clock model; accepted to avoid catch-up spirals. Offline auto-DPS-only is deliberately worse than active play.
**Revisit:** If hidden-tab time must count exactly, move the offline replay to a `visibilitychange → visible` hook (reusing the same bounded replay) rather than changing the engine.

## 2026-09-25 — engine-core is consumed as TypeScript source via `exports`
**Context:** Phase 1 needs the three workspaces to install and typecheck, and web/sim must import `engine-core` without a build step. A source-consumed workspace package still has to resolve under `moduleResolution: Bundler`.
**Choice:** `engine-core/package.json` maps `"exports"` to `./src/index.ts` (root) and `./save/index.ts` (subpath), each with a `"types"` condition first, plus matching `"main"`/`"types"` fields. The `build` script is `tsc --noEmit` (typecheck-only; no emitted JS for this prototype). `web/src/main.ts` carries a side-effect `import "@auto-auto-clicker/engine-core"` in Phase 1 so `npm run typecheck` actually exercises cross-workspace resolution instead of trivially passing.
**Trade-offs:** Consumers must be bundler/transpiler-based (Vite, tsx) since `node` cannot execute the `.ts` entry directly. Acceptable: both hosts are bundlers and the build step is intentionally absent for the prototype.
**Revisit:** If engine-core needs to be published or consumed by plain Node, add a real emit build and point `exports` at `dist/`.

## 2026-09-25 — Monorepo layout and workspaces
**Context:** Greenfield project with three distinct deliverables (pure engine, web renderer, headless sim).  
**Choice:** Use npm workspaces with packages `engine-core`, `web`, and `sim`. Shared TypeScript base config at repo root; each workspace owns its `package.json` and entry points.  
**Trade-offs:** Adds a small root scaffolding step, but keeps the engine package independently publishable/testable and avoids relative-path import hacks.  
**Revisit:** If the project grows a backend or a fourth host (Expo), re-evaluate whether pnpm/turborepo is justified.

## 2026-09-25 — engine-core pure tick API
**Context:** The engine must be platform-agnostic, testable headlessly, and driveable by any host (web, RN, sim).  
**Choice:** Export pure functions `advance(state, deltaMs)` and `applyAction(state, action)`. `advance` owns time-based simulation only; `applyAction` owns explicit player commands. Content/balance catalog lives inside `engine-core` as module-level constants, not inside save state. Hosts drive `advance` and split offline time into bounded fixed-size steps themselves.  
**Trade-offs:** Hosts must implement the loop and offline chunking; renderer cannot directly mutate state.  
**Revisit:** Never for this project; changing this would break the platform-agnostic goal.

## 2026-09-25 — Save-state shape and repository abstraction
**Context:** Saves start local (localStorage) but must migrate to Supabase jsonb later without engine changes.  
**Choice:** Save is a versioned `SaveGame` blob: `{ version, savedAt, state }`. Content definitions (`GearDefinition`, `EnemyDefinition`) are kept separate from per-player `GameState`. `engine-core/save` exports a `SaveRepository` interface plus a `LocalStorageSaveRepository` implementation that reads `globalThis.localStorage`. A future `SupabaseSaveRepository` will implement the same interface in the host layer.  
**Trade-offs:** Engine gains a browser-global dependency in one adapter file, but no DOM/React imports and no network code. Save blob must be migrated on version changes.  
**Revisit:** Only when changing the save schema version.

## 2026-09-25 — Determinism and numeric precision
**Context:** Headless sim must be reproducible and offline progress must not drift.  
**Choice:** All combat/economy numbers are integers. Auto-damage is calculated as `floor((dps * deltaMs + carry) / 1000)` with a per-state damage carry that is a fraction in `[0, 1)`. RNG is a seeded mulberry32 whose state is stored in `GameState.meta.rngState`.  
**Trade-offs:** Formulas use `Math.pow` then `Math.floor`; results could differ by 1 across JS engines near integer boundaries, mitigated by floor and conservative tolerances.  
**Revisit:** If porting to a host with a very different JS engine (e.g., old Hermes), re-test integer boundaries.

## 2026-09-25 — Pacing thresholds and economy baseline
**Context:** Pacing must be provable by headless sim, not manual play.  
**Choice:** Soft boss check = first `BossCheckFailed` event (projected boss kill > 60 s). Hard progression wall = first `ProgressionWall` event (projected kill > 600 s). Baseline balance constants are defined in `/engine-core/src/balance.ts` (e.g., `hpGrowth = 1.5`, `weaponLevelExp = 1.4`, `upgradeStatMultiplier = 1.18`).  
**Trade-offs:** Targets are sensitive to these constants; sim will validate and expose tuning knobs.  
**Revisit:** After sim results; only constants change, not the schema or API.
**Superseded by:** "Deterministic power core: bounded RNG, bounded free grants, DPS-based swap" (Phase 3b) — the baseline constants changed; only the schema/API claim still holds.

## 2026-09-25 — Web renderer stack
**Context:** Renderer is disposable; the permanent artifact is engine-core.  
**Choice:** Web uses Vite + TypeScript + vanilla DOM. It holds zero balance numbers, drop tables, or state mutation; it only calls `engine-core` functions and renders returned state/events.  
**Trade-offs:** No component framework means slightly more manual DOM, but zero framework dependency and a thin boundary.  
**Revisit:** If the Expo port wants to reuse web code, migrate to a framework; not needed now.

## 2026-09-25 — All tuning constants live in `balance.ts`, re-exported by `index.ts`
**Context:** The public constants (`BOSS_TIMER_MS`, `HARD_WALL_PROJECTED_KILL_MS`, `ACTIVE_CLICKS_PER_SECOND`, `CURRENT_SAVE_VERSION`, `BAG_CAP`) are read by sim code that also imports the economy formulas. Defining them in `index.ts` would create import cycles (`advance` → `index` → `advance`).
**Choice:** Define every constant and formula in `src/balance.ts`, then re-export the constants from `src/index.ts`. Consumers see the exact same names and values; there is still exactly one file to tune.
**Trade-offs:** `index.ts` contains re-exports rather than literal `const` declarations, so a reader must follow the re-export to find a value.
**Revisit:** Never; adding a second tuner file would defeat the single-knob design.

## 2026-09-25 — Tick/choice edge semantics for `advance` and `resolveChoice`
**Context:** The brief specifies multi-enemy ticks and three choice outcomes but leaves ordering ambiguous.
**Choice:** In one tick, integer auto-damage is consumed sequentially across enemies (kill, spawn next, continue with the remainder); leftover damage is discarded when a pending choice blocks progression. `resolveChoice('iap')` clears the original pending choice, then performs a real kill whose own stage-entry checks may raise a fresh pending choice for the next stage; `'wait'`/`'watchAd'` only grant gold and never re-run stage-entry checks in the same call.
**Trade-offs:** A single enormous `deltaMs` can traverse many stages and emit many events; overkill past the final kill in a tick is lost rather than banked.
**Revisit:** Only if the sim shows banking overkill is needed for pacing accuracy.

## 2026-09-25 — Async `SaveRepository` + structural localStorage adapter
**Context:** Saves start local but must later move to Supabase; engine-core's tsconfig has no DOM lib.
**Choice:** `SaveRepository` is `Promise`-based (`load(): Promise<SaveGame | null>`, `save(): Promise<void>`). `LocalStorageSaveRepository` declares a minimal structural `StorageLike` interface and reads `(globalThis as { localStorage?: StorageLike }).localStorage`; when absent, `load()` resolves null and `save()` rejects with a clear error. `saveGame`/`loadGame` (with version check) live in `src/state.ts`. `@types/node` is an engine-core devDependency, used only by the fs-based boundary test.
**Trade-offs:** No `clear()` method yet (not required this phase); the async surface costs an `await` even for localStorage.
**Revisit:** When the Supabase adapter lands, revisit whether `clear()` and conflict/version metadata belong on the interface.

## 2026-09-25 — Headless pacing simulator (Phase 3)
**Context:** The pacing target must be proven by an automated harness, not manual play. Phase 3 needed a deterministic, wall-clock-free driver of the pure engine.
**Choice:** `sim/src/sim.ts` advances a virtual clock in fixed 100 ms steps up to 2 h, clicks every 500 ms (`1000 / ACTIVE_CLICKS_PER_SECOND`), resolves any pending choice on the free `wait` path, and runs a greedy economy after each tick (upgrade the equipped weapon while affordable, then equip the best strictly-higher-`itemLevel` bag weapon). It records the first `bossCheckFailed` and first `progressionWall` from the tick that sets `choices.pending`. Canonical seed `12345` is the hard assertion (soft 6.0 min ±20%, hard 54.0 min ±20%); four extra seeds are informational warnings only. Exit code 1 on canonical failure, 0 on pass.
**Trade-offs:** The sim must mirror a real player's policy, so it cannot assume perfect play; the greedy itemLevel swap forfeits upgrade levels, which makes outcomes highly sensitive to drop RNG.
**Revisit:** If sweep-seed robustness becomes a requirement, revisit the sim's swap policy (itemLevel vs stat comparison), never the tolerance.
**Superseded by:** "Deterministic power core: bounded RNG, bounded free grants, DPS-based swap" (Phase 3b) — the swap policy is now DPS-based and all seeds are hard assertions.

## 2026-09-25 — Pacing tuned via economy levers (Phase 3)
**Context:** The canonical seed initially produced soft 3.92 min and hard 10.39 min — both far short of 6.0/54.0 min. Tuning had to stay inside `balance.ts` and keep every engine test green, including behavioral pacing scenarios.
**Choice:** Two committed lever changes: `gear.upgradeStatMultiplier` 1.18 → 1.3, then `gear.upgradeCostGrowth` 1.6 → 1.35. Canonical seed result: soft 6.58 min (delta +9.7%), hard 58.99 min (+9.2%) — both inside ±20%. `hpGrowth` and `levelExponent` were deliberately rejected: `choices.test.ts` asserts behavioral projection windows for the stage-10 boss (geared via `makeGear(5)`, and unarmed), which only hold on the baseline enemy-HP curve; weakening enemies by lowering `hpGrowth` or raising `levelExponent` breaks those behavioral assertions, and the rules forbid loosening behavioral tests.
**Trade-offs:** Upgrades are now cheaper and stronger, which inflates late-game stats; sweep seeds still spread over ~13–37 min hard wall because drop RNG couples non-linearly with the greedy policy. Canonical is the only hard assertion, so this is reported as a warning.
**Revisit:** Only if tolerance policy or sweep robustness requirements change.
**Superseded by:** "Deterministic power core: bounded RNG, bounded free grants, DPS-based swap" (Phase 3b).
