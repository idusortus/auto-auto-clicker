- 2026-09-25: Phase 3 sim (`sim/src/sim.ts`) drives the engine at 100 ms steps with a 500 ms click cadence. It records `bossCheckFailed`/`progressionWall` from the tick that sets `choices.pending`, resolves pending on the free `wait` path, and runs a greedy economy each tick. RCA note: `resolveChoice('wait')` grants `goldReward(stage)*7.5`, and `goldReward` grows as `1.45^stage`, so a single free choice injects enormous gold at high stages — this makes the economy explode and pacing highly sensitive to constants.
