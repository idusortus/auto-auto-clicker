## 2026-09-27 — Phase 2: economy retune restores the pacing proof (DeepSeek V4.1 Flash)
Retuned the single file allowed (`engine-core/src/balance.ts`) to bring `npm run sim` back to exit 0 after Phase 1's rings/necklaces pushed it out of tolerance. The brief's working theory was that crit/necklace power front-loads progression; instrumentation showed the opposite cause. The sim's DPS-based equip policy **never equips a ring or necklace** (they carry no `dps`/`clickDamage`, so their candidate DPS is the unarmed base 6 and can never beat an equipped weapon — `slots[none]` on every run), so the crit/necklace factors are **sim-inert**. Zeroing them reproduces the failure exactly (old weights: 7.22/47.25) and the fix exactly (new weights: 6.55/55.46). The real regression was the thinned weapon stream: with ring weights 0.25 apiece only ~66% of drops were weapons, so the equipped weapon lagged the stage by ~2 item levels and clears slowed (soft LATE). Fix: `SLOT_DROP_WEIGHTS` rings 0.25/0.25 → 0.04/0.04, weapon 1.0 and necklace 0.02 unchanged → ~91% weapon share. Result: all 5 seeds PASS (soft 6.51–6.60 min, hard 50.95–55.46 min; canonical soft +9.1%, hard +2.7%), drops-primary net 99.6–100.0% / gross 87.4–87.7%, eq/stage 0.78–0.94, soft autoDps ~1.7e3 (≪1e10). No tolerance, timer, threshold, or assertion changed; no test edit needed (76/76 green). Ring/necklace factors were deliberately left unchanged: unprovable by the harness, and their exponential `gearGrowth^(itemLevel-1)` scaling on multiplicative bonuses is explosive for a real player who equips them (item level 50 ring ≈ 2×10⁴ crit-multiplier contribution) — flagged as a deferred structural decision in `decisions.md`/`STATE.md`.
